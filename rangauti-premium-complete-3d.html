ABOUT RANGAUTI

A peaceful retreat in the heart of Moulvibazar.

Nestled near Manu Bridge on Taltola, Kulaura Road, Moulvibazar, Rangauti Resort is a tranquil retreat where the beauty of nature meets modern comfort.

Surrounded by peaceful natural landscapes, the resort offers comfortable rooms and suites, a refreshing swimming pool, dining facilities and enjoyable activities designed to make every visit relaxing and memorable.
