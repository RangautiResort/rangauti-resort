  2. Check-in: 1:15 PM. Check-out: 11:45 AM.
  3. Breakfast: 8:30 AM – 10:30 AM.
  4. Lunch: 1:30 PM – 3:30 PM.
  5. Dinner: 8:30 PM – 10:30 PM.
  6. Outside Food: Outside food is not allowed inside the resort.
  7. Cancellation: Free cancellation is available up to 72 hours before the check-in time.
  8. Extra Person: BDT 1,500 per person.
  9. Child Policy: Children above 6 years are charged as adults.
  10. Couple Policy: Only married couples are allowed. Valid ID and marriage proof may be required.
  11. Room Booking: 50% advance payment is required to confirm a room booking.
  12. Lunch Package: For 5 or more persons, 50% advance payment is required. For fewer than 5 persons, full payment is required.
  13. Day Long Package: Advance payment is required to confirm the booking.
  14. Beverage, Entry & Swimming Packages: No advance payment is required. Payment can be made at the resort.
  15. All bookings are subject to availability and prior confirmation.
ABOUT RANGAUTI

A Peaceful Escape
in Moulvibazar

Nestled near Manu Bridge, Taltola, Kulaura Road, Moulvibazar, Rangauti Resort offers a peaceful escape with comfortable rooms, a refreshing swimming pool, dining facilities, and enjoyable activities.

Whether you’re visiting with family, friends, or loved ones, we’re here to make your stay comfortable, relaxing, and memorable.

Experience Nature. Enjoy Comfort. Create Memories. 🌿

Our resort features comfortable rooms and suites, swimming pool, restaurant and dining facilities, natural surroundings and enjoyable activities including Boat Ride.

We also offer Day Long, Lunch and other visitor packages for families, groups and special occasions.

Whether you are planning a relaxing stay, a family outing or a special celebration, Rangauti Resort welcomes you to enjoy nature, comfort and memorable moments.

Experience nature, comfort and memorable moments at Rangauti Resort, Moulvibazar, Sylhet.
