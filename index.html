Terms & Conditions

Please read our booking conditions carefully.

  1. Resort Visiting Time: 11:00 AM – 6:00 PM. This applies to visitors, including Lunch Package, Beverage, Snacks and Day Long Package guests.
  2. Check-in: 1:15 PM.
  3. Check-out: 11:45 AM.
  4. Outside Food: Outside food is not permitted inside the resort.
  5. Cancellation: Free cancellation is available up to 72 hours before the check-in time.
  6. Extra Person: BDT 1,500 per additional person.
  7. Child Policy: Children above 6 years will be charged as adults.
  8. Couple Policy: Only married couples are allowed; valid ID/marriage proof may be required.
  9. Availability: All bookings are subject to availability and prior confirmation.
  10. Special Requests: Special requests are subject to availability.
  11. Additional Services: Charges for restaurant orders, boat rides and other additional services may apply separately.
  12. Resort Rules: Guests are required to follow all resort rules and instructions for the safety, comfort and convenience of all guests.
  13. Damage Policy: Guests will be responsible for any damage, loss, or breakage of resort property caused by them during their stay. Applicable repair or replacement charges may be charged accordingly.
  14. Package-Specific Payment Terms:
    • Room Booking: 50% advance payment is required to confirm the booking.
    • Lunch Package: For 5 persons or more, 50% advance payment is required. For fewer than 5 persons, full payment is required.
    • Day Long Package: Advance payment is required to confirm the booking.
    • Beverage, Entry & Swimming Packages: No advance payment is required. Payment can be made at the resort.

About Rangauti Resort

A peaceful destination for nature, comfort and memorable moments.

Rangauti Resort is a beautiful leisure destination in Moulvibazar, Sylhet, where guests can enjoy a peaceful environment surrounded by nature. Away from the busy routine of everyday life, the resort offers a comfortable and relaxing place for families, friends and loved ones to spend quality time together.

Our resort features comfortable rooms and suites, a swimming pool, restaurant and dining facilities, beautiful natural surroundings and enjoyable resort activities. Guests can relax by the pool, enjoy delicious meals, experience the beauty of nature and create memorable moments with their loved ones.

Rangauti Resort is ideal for family vacations, group outings, day-long visits, special occasions and peaceful getaways. Our goal is to provide every guest with a comfortable, enjoyable and memorable resort experience.
